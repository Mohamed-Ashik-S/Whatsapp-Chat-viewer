Open an exported chat

Tap Add chats and pick one or more WhatsApp exports (.zip or .txt). You can add as many chats as you like.

  1. In WhatsApp, open a chat.
  2. Menu, More, Export chat.
  3. Choose Include media or Without media, then save the file.

Everything stays on this phone. The app has no internet access.
